## Programming Fundamentals III Course Sequence

Status: Partial approved sequence
Course: COSC 2436
Last updated: 2026-11-12

### Purpose
This file records approved sequence relationships without inventing a complete curriculum.

### Approved relationships
- Binary search tree behavior and height precede or accompany balanced-tree motivation.
- Priority-queue behavior and familiar implementation trade-offs precede binary-heap representation.
- Binary-heap representation, invariants, and operations precede std::priority_queue use.
- Structure-selection comparisons recur after individual structures are understood.
- A short structure-selection capstone belongs near the end of the course.
