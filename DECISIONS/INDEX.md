# Accepted Decisions Index

Decision records preserve rationale for consequential accepted choices. Load a decision when reconsidering a choice, changing an implementing rule, comparing alternatives, checking assumptions, or evaluating revisit conditions.

## Decision records

- `pedagogical_progression.md`: cross-course pedagogical progression.
- `quiz_import_and_metadata.md`: D2L quiz import, title, difficulty, ordering, and randomization convention.
- `repository_licensing.md`: repository licensing choice.
- `cpp_namespace_convention.md`: cross-course namespace convention for instructional C++.
- `semantic_visual_mapping.md`: cross-course semantic mapping across dark and light backgrounds.
- `hierarchical_indexing.md`: repository-wide hierarchical navigation model.

## Creation guidance

- `decision_record_structure.md`: criteria and structure for new decision records.

Do not load every decision for routine artifact generation when its accepted operational conclusion is already present in governing standards or source-of-truth files.
