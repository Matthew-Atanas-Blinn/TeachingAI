# Decision Record Structure

Create a decision record for a consequential accepted choice when future users may reasonably ask why the system works that way or when alternatives have meaningful tradeoffs.

Recommended structure:

```markdown
# Decision: Short title

Status: Accepted
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Scope: Repository | Cross-course | Course | Module | Artifact type

## Context
## Decision
## Benefits
## Drawbacks
## Assumptions
## Alternatives considered
## Consequences
## Revisit when
```

Do not create a record for every minor edit. Keep operational rules in the appropriate governing standards or source-of-truth files. Decision records preserve rationale; they do not replace implementation rules.
