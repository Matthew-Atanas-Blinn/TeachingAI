## Data-Structure Selection Decisions

### Status

Partial decision record. The curricular direction below is approved, but the capstone module and assessment details have not yet been fully designed.

### Approved decision

Reinforce data-structure selection briefly while individual structures are taught and culminate with a small capstone module near the end of Programming III. Give selection a greater share of final-exam questions than earlier assessments. Students should justify choices using required operations, frequency or importance of operations, ordering requirements, duplicate handling when relevant, expected operation complexity, and at least one plausible alternative. Some scenarios may appropriately use more than one structure.

### Undecided details

Capstone length and format, implementation requirements, exact compared structures, workload-analysis depth, final-exam weighting, question formats, grading criteria, and combined-structure expectations remain undecided. These details must be settled before substantial structure-selection materials are created.
