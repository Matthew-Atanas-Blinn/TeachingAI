## Data-Structure Selection Module

### Status

Decision stub only. This module has not yet been fully designed.

### Approved direction

Data-structure selection will be reinforced through small comparisons throughout Programming III and concluded with a short capstone module. The final examination should give selection a greater share of questions than earlier assessments. Students should identify required operations, choose and justify a structure, reject at least one plausible alternative, and recognize when combined structures are appropriate.

### Required before substantial module development

Complete the objectives, compared structures, expected complexity depth, scenarios, capstone format, final-exam weighting, acceptable reasoning, grading criteria, review checklist, and source of truth before creating substantial module or exam materials.

### Routing

Use this folder for data-structure comparison, selection scenarios, the capstone, and final-exam questions requiring students to choose and justify a structure.
