## Balanced Trees Decisions

### Status

Partial decision record. The direction below is approved, but the complete module has not yet been designed.

### Approved decision

Balanced trees should be discussed when binary search trees are introduced. Establish that BST operations are O(h), that unfavorable insertion order can produce h = O(n), and that balancing controls height. Students should conceptually understand why degeneration matters, how rotations preserve BST ordering while changing shape, and why ordered library containers can offer stronger guarantees than an unbalanced student BST. The default depth is conceptual explanation and representative rotation tracing, not a complete AVL or red-black implementation.

### Undecided details

The primary balancing scheme, exact rotations, implementation depth, assessment format, library-implementation discussion, and slide/assignment integration remain undecided. These details must be completed before substantial balanced-tree materials are created.
